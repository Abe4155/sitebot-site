Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

PhoneShopPOS is point-of-sale software for cell phone repair and device buyback shops. It also offers free tools for printing invoices, receipts and work orders. It is operated by PhoneShopPOS. When this policy says "PhoneShopPOS," "we," "us," or "our," it means PhoneShopPOS and its operators.

Our contact email is info@phoneshoppos.com. Our website is phoneshoppos.com.

2. What we collect

Free printing tools

The invoice, receipt and work order tools on phoneshoppos.com work without an account. What you type into them is not sent to us. Your shop name and phone number may be remembered in your browser on that device so you do not have to type them again.

Accounts

When you create a PhoneShopPOS account we collect the details needed to run it, such as your name, shop name, email address and login. We also store the records you enter while using the software, including customers, repair tickets, buyback receipts and sales.

Billing

If you subscribe to a paid plan, we collect the billing information needed to charge for it.

Information collected automatically

When you visit phoneshoppos.com we may collect standard web analytics data including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages. This is used to understand how the site is being used and improve it.

Communications

If you email us or contact us directly we retain those communications to follow up and improve our service.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and operate your account
  • Store, display and print the records you enter
  • Process payments for paid plans
  • Send you account notices and updates about PhoneShopPOS features and pricing
  • Respond to your questions and support requests
  • Improve the software based on how it is being used
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not build profiles for third-party ad targeting. We do not sell your data.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We share information only with the service providers needed to run PhoneShopPOS, such as our hosting provider (Vercel hosts phoneshoppos.com), our payment processor, and the services we use to send account email.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data. We choose providers carefully and only share what is necessary for the service to function.

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of PhoneShopPOS, its operators, or others.

5. Information about your customers

When you use PhoneShopPOS you may enter information about your own customers, such as names, phone numbers, device details, and the identification you record for a buyback. This information belongs to you as the shop owner. PhoneShopPOS stores and processes it on your behalf so you can run your shop.

As a shop owner using PhoneShopPOS you are responsible for:

  • Telling your customers how you use the information you collect from them
  • Only collecting and using customer information for legitimate business purposes, such as repairs, buybacks, sales and the records the law requires you to keep
  • Complying with applicable privacy laws in your jurisdiction regarding customer data

PhoneShopPOS does not use your customers' information for its own marketing purposes.

6. Cookies, local storage and analytics

PhoneShopPOS uses cookies or similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use the site. The free printing tools use your browser's local storage to remember details like your shop name on your device. We do not use advertising cookies or share analytics data with ad networks.

Most browsers allow you to control cookies and local storage through their settings. Disabling them may affect how some parts of the site function, such as staying signed in.

We do not currently use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics platform that sells or shares your browsing data with advertisers.

7. How long we keep it

Account data is retained while your account is active and for up to 12 months after you close it, for billing records and dispute resolution purposes.

You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing info@phoneshoppos.com. We will confirm deletion within 30 days except where we are required by law to retain certain records.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Correct any information that is inaccurate
  • Delete your information from our systems
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send

To exercise any of these rights email us at info@phoneshoppos.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are located in California you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom you may have additional rights under the GDPR. Contact us if you have questions about your specific rights.

9. Children

PhoneShopPOS is business software intended for adults who own or operate cell phone repair and buyback businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor please contact us at info@phoneshoppos.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy as PhoneShopPOS evolves. When we do we will update the date at the top of this page. If changes are significant we will notify account holders by email before the changes take effect.

Continued use of PhoneShopPOS after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
