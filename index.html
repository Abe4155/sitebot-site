Repair tickets and buyback receipts for phone shops.

PhoneShopPOS is point-of-sale software for cell phone repair and buyback shops. Check in a repair and print a ticket with a tear-off tag that stays with the phone. Buy a device and print a receipt with the seller's ID and a signature line. Ring up accessories with sales tax. Every ticket, buyback and sale is saved under the customer's phone number, so when they walk back in you can pull up their history in seconds. It runs in the browser on any computer or tablet, with nothing to install and no contract.

What it does

Repair tickets

  • Check in a device in under a minute
  • Tear-off tag goes on the phone
  • Pending, ready and picked up in one list
  • Deposits now, balance at pickup

Buyback receipts

  • Seller name, phone and ID
  • IMEI and condition for each device
  • Signature line on every receipt
  • Pay out by cash, card, Cash App or Zelle

Sales

  • Line items and sales tax
  • Cash drawer and end-of-day count
  • Reprint any receipt
  • Customer history by phone number
